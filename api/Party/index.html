Party

Party state that persists across servers, stored in MemoryStore.

Use this when a party must survive its members being on different servers. To queue a group of players who are already together on one server, Matchmaker:enqueueGroup takes userIds directly and does not touch any of this. A Matchmaker builds its parties instance on first access, and nothing is written to the store until a method is called. 

local parties = queue.parties

local party = Muster.Result.unwrap(parties:create(leaderUserId))
parties:invite(leaderUserId, friendUserId)

-- On the friend's server, which may be a different one:
local invites = Muster.Result.unwrap(parties:listInvites(friendUserId))
parties:acceptInvite(friendUserId, invites[1].id)

Rules

  • A player belongs to at most one party. Membership is a single-slot index per userId written with compare-and-set, so two servers adding the same player concurrently produce exactly one winner; the loser gets AlreadyInParty.
  • Parties hold up to maxMembers (default 8; a Matchmaker passes its size.max).
  • Only the leader may invite, kick, transfer leadership, disband, or queue the party.
  • When the leader leaves, the longest-standing remaining member inherits. Removing the last member deletes the party.
  • Invites live in an inbox keyed by the target userId, capped at 20 and expiring after 60 seconds.

While queued

A queued party is locked: invite, leave, kick, transferLeader and disband all return PartyLocked until the ticket matches, is cancelled, or expires. This keeps the roster stable between the matcher reading it and the match committing.

If the server holding a queued party dies, its ticket stops being heartbeaten. The lock is then cleared the next time the party is read, rather than persisting for the party's full two-hour lifetime.

Records

Parties expire after 2 hours without activity. Accepting an invite first takes a 20-second Reserved membership slot and only promotes it to Active once the party record has been updated, so a failure between the two leaves a slot that clears itself rather than a player stuck in neither state.

leaderUserId: number,

) → Result<PartyInvite>--

NotLeader, PartyLocked, PartyFull, or InvalidTarget for a self-invite.

Invites a player to the caller's party.

The invite is written to an inbox keyed by the target's userId, so they can read it from whichever server they are on. Inboxes hold 20 invites, evicting the oldest, and entries expire after 60 seconds. A second invite from the same party replaces the first.
