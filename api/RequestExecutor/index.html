RequestExecutor

Wraps every store and messaging call in a retry policy, tracks per-structure health, and trips a circuit breaker on structures that keep failing.

Requests carry a priority, where lower is more important:

Priority Used by Attempts Circuit-broken?
1 The commit path — claims, the Ready CAS 5 Never
2 Leases 5 Never
3 Ticket writes, party mutations 5 Never
4-6 Lane index writes, range scans, reads 3 Yes

The split matters: shedding a lane scan under load is fine and self-corrects on the next pass, but abandoning a half-committed match leaves tickets stuck until their claim expires. Critical work always gets to try.

Throttling is detected by matching the error text, because that is the only signal Roblox exposes.

isThrottled​

</>
RequestExecutor.isThrottled(self: RequestExecutor) → boolean

True once at least 1% of all observed requests came back throttled. The engine reacts by declining to lease new lanes and doubling its scan delay, shedding parallelism before it slows the critical path.
